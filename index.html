Six times a day, one vow.

Seis is a book you keep six times a day. At each time you stop for a minute with one vow: a plus, a minus, and one small to-do. The box is kept when the to-do is done.

The page

Six boxes, then the evening.

The page is the one practitioners have kept on paper for years. Seis keeps it on your phone, at your times, and remembers the one thing paper cannot: whether you did the to-do.

+ −

A plus and a minus

One specific thing you did that kept the vow, and one that did not. Small and true beats grand and vague.

○

A to-do

Something good for that vow, five minutes or less, within two days. Three ideas sit under every box, so “small” has examples.

☾

The evening

The best three and worst three things of the whole day, a note on meditation, and the reminders you carry into tomorrow.

Kept, not just written

The box is kept when the to-do is done.

A box has three states: empty, written, kept. Open to-dos wait at the top of Today, from today and the two days before. Tap the ring when one is done. Swipe to let one go. Nothing piles up.

Late start

Up late? Start from now.

Slept past the first boxes? Today offers to begin the six from now, your usual spacing apart, with the last box by 11:00 PM. One tap, and the day is yours again.

A Shortcuts action, “Start Today’s Book Now”, can do it the moment your alarm is dismissed.

The vows

Six vows a day, in order, then around again.

The ten virtues and the twelve refuge commitments are in from the start. Add the Bodhisattva vows when you are ready, or write vows of your own.

The Book shows every day as six dots filled when the box is kept.

The evening

Close the day with the best three.

The lifetime vow of the day at the top. The day’s to-dos, each with its switch. Then the best three, the worst three, a note on meditation, and the special reminders that carry over to tomorrow.

Private by design

Your book stays with you.

A book of confessions is nobody else’s business. Seis is built so that it cannot be.

No account. No server.

Everything is kept in the app’s own folder on your phone. There is nothing to sign in to, no analytics, no third-party code.

Lock it with Face ID.

One switch in Settings. The app locks when it goes to the background, and the app switcher shows only the mark, never a page.

Quiet reminders.

Six a day, one per box, scheduled on your phone. A reminder names the vow and how many to-dos are open; it never shows what you wrote. Export a copy of the whole book whenever you like.
